## Reading the hit rate

Exactly six of the 40 commits change none of the step's inputs (docs/,
site/ and README.md: Jest's `roots: ["test"]` keeps its crawl to test/ and
the src/ files the tests import), and exactly those six hit; every other
commit changes a recorded input. Of the 34 misses, 23 change `src/*.js`
(several are JSDoc-only edits, which a step-level memoizer cannot tell
from code changes), 2 change tests, 4 change package.json and 3 bump the
version or lockfile (npm ci rewrites `node_modules/.package-lock.json`),
1 changes jest.config.js, plus the first commit. The 2025 commits (1-20)
fail 11 locale tests under Node 24's ICU (luxon's CI then pinned older
Nodes): those results are failures, recorded and replayed like passes.

Hits replay in 1.4-4.8s; the 34 misses each pay the observer (+24%
execution in the overhead bench) and the per-run fixed costs on a 4-12s
suite, which six hits do not repay at this load.
